1. Introduction

The Silver Sands HRMS is a web-based system designed to help manage human resources efficiently. It allows users to track employee attendance, manage employee records, generate reports, and maintain a secure audit trail.

Purpose of the System

This system replaces manual attendance tracking and paper-based record keeping with a modern digital solution. It provides real-time visibility into employee attendance patterns and simplifies payroll preparation.

Who Can Use This System?

  • HR Staff — Full access to manage employees and attendance
  • Supervisors — Can manage their team's attendance and view reports
  • Viewers — Read-only access to view employee data and reports
  • System Administrators — Full system control and user management
Important Note

All user actions are logged for audit purposes. The system automatically logs out inactive users after 10 minutes for security reasons.

2. Login & Access

How to Log In

1
Open the System URL

Open your web browser and navigate to the HRMS URL provided by IT.

2
Enter Your Credentials

Enter your username and password in the login form. Your credentials are provided by HR or IT.

3
Click Login

Click the "Login" button. If successful, you will be redirected to the Dashboard.

Important Security Notice
  • Do not share your password with anyone
  • Log out when you finish using the system
  • You cannot be logged in on multiple devices simultaneously
  • If you forget your password, contact HR or IT for assistance

Automatic Logout

The system will automatically log you out after 10 minutes of inactivity. A warning banner will appear 60 seconds before logout, giving you the option to stay active.

To stay active: Click the "Stay Active" button when the warning appears, or simply click anywhere on the page.

3. Dashboard

The Dashboard is your starting point when you log in. It provides a quick overview of attendance data for the current payroll period.

Dashboard Features

  • Statistics Cards — Shows total Present (P), Absent (A), Sick (S), Leave (L), and Day Off (O) for all employees in the current period.
  • Department Summary Table — Breaks down attendance by department with staff counts and totals.
  • Quick Info — Shows total employees, active departments, period days, and the current payroll period.
  • Quick Actions — The "Open Attendance Register" button takes you directly to the attendance page.
Tip

Use the Dashboard to get a quick snapshot of attendance without navigating to other pages. The data updates automatically when you refresh.

4. Employee Management

The Employee Management page allows you to view, add, edit, and delete employee records. This is typically managed by HR staff and supervisors.

Viewing Employees

  • All employees are displayed in a table with columns for number, employee number, name, department, position, join date, and day off.
  • Use the Search box to find employees by name, employee number, or position.
  • Use the Department Filter to view employees in a specific department.
  • Use the Day Off Filter to find employees with a specific scheduled day off.

Adding an Employee

1
Click "Add Employee"

Click the "Add Employee" button at the top right of the page.

2
Fill in Employee Details

Enter the employee's full name, department, position, join date, and day off (if applicable).

3
Save

Click "Save Employee" to add the employee to the system.

Note on Employee Numbers

Employee numbers are automatically generated by the system and cannot be manually changed.

Editing an Employee

  • Click the Edit button (pencil icon) next to the employee's name.
  • Update the required fields in the modal that appears.
  • Click "Save Employee" to apply changes.

Deleting an Employee

  • Click the Delete button (trash icon) next to the employee's name.
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted.
  • Important: This action cannot be undone and will also delete all attendance records for that employee.
Important

Only users with proper permissions (HR, Supervisors, IT) can add, edit, or delete employees. Viewers can only view employee information.

5. Attendance Register

The Attendance Register is the core feature of the HRMS. It allows you to track daily attendance for all employees.

Selecting a Period

  • Use the Payroll Period dropdown to select the period you want to view.
  • Periods run from the 27th of the previous month to the 26th of the selected month.
  • Click "New Period" to create a new payroll period.
  • Click "Mark Day Offs" to automatically mark scheduled day offs for employees.

Marking Attendance

  • The attendance table shows employees grouped by department.
  • Each column represents a day in the period.
  • Click on any cell to change the attendance status.
  • Available statuses: P Present, A Absent, S Sick, L Leave, O Day Off
Auto-Save Feature

Changes are automatically saved after 600ms (less than a second). You don't need to click a save button. Look for the "Auto-save: ON" indicator in the period selector.

Understanding the Table

  • Department Rows — Gold/bold rows showing department names.
  • Employee Rows — Each row shows an employee's attendance for all days.
  • Total Columns — The last five columns show totals for P, A, S, L, and O.
  • Subtotal Rows — Show department totals for the period.
  • Today Highlight — Today's date column is highlighted in gold.
Tip

If an employee has a scheduled day off, the system automatically marks "O" for those days. You can still override this if needed.

6. Employee Summary

The Employee Summary page provides a detailed breakdown of attendance totals (P, A, S, L, O) for each employee.

Views

  • Pay Period View — Shows summary for a specific payroll period.
  • Date Range View — Shows summary for a custom date range of your choice.

Features

  • Employee Filter — Select a specific employee to view their individual stats.
  • Click to View — Click on any employee row to view their individual summary.
  • Department Grouping — Employees are grouped by department with subtotals.
  • Grand Totals — Overall totals for all employees.
  • Export CSV — Download the summary data as a CSV file for use in Excel.
When to Use This Page

Use this page to quickly see which employees have high absence rates, or to verify attendance totals before payroll processing.

Individual Employee View

When you select a specific employee, you will see:

  • Employee details (name, number, department, position, join date)
  • Large stat cards showing P, A, S, L, O totals
  • Total days worked
  • The period or date range being viewed

7. Reports

The Reports page allows you to generate professional Excel reports with color-coded attendance data.

Generating a Report

1
Select a Period

Choose the payroll period you want to report on from the dropdown.

2
Click "Generate Report"

Click the gold button to generate the report.

3
Download the File

The Excel file will download automatically. It is password-protected for security.

Report Features

  • Color-Coded Attendance — P (green), A (red), S (orange), L (blue), O (grey)
  • Department Grouping — Departments are grouped with subtotals
  • Employee Totals — Each row shows totals for P, A, S, L, O
  • Grand Totals — Overall totals at the bottom of the report
  • Password Protection — Report is read-only and requires a password to modify
Note

Only HR staff and supervisors can generate reports. Viewers cannot access this feature.

8. Sign-In Sheet

The Sign-In Sheet page generates a printable daily attendance register for employees to sign in and out.

Generating a Sign-In Sheet

1
Select a Date

Choose the date for which you want to generate a sign-in sheet.

2
Click "Generate Sign-In Sheet"

The sign-in sheet will be displayed on the page.

3
Print the Sheet

Click the "Print" button to print the sign-in sheet. The print view is optimized for paper.

Sign-In Sheet Features

  • Company Header — Silver Sands Salima logo and title
  • Employee Listing — All employees grouped by department
  • Time Tracking — Morning and afternoon time-in/time-out fields
  • Signature Column — Space for employees to sign
  • Instructions — Guidance on how to fill in the sheet
Tip

The sign-in sheet is designed for daily use. Print it at the beginning of the day and have employees complete it as they arrive and leave.

Viewing Your Profile

  • Your session status is shown (Active).
  • Your avatar shows the first letter of your name.

Editing Your Profile

Click "Edit Profile"

This opens the profile edit modal.

2
Update Your Details

Change Password (Optional)

Save Changes

10. Security & User Management

The User Management page allows authorized users to manage system accounts.

User Roles

Role Description Access Level
IT Specialist Full system access, can manage users and view all activity logs Highest
System Admin Can manage employees and attendance High
Supervisor Can manage employees, attendance, and create user accounts Medium
Viewer Read-only access to view data Low

Creating a New User

  • Click the "Create Account" button (visible to authorized users only).
  • Fill in username, full name, password, role, and department.
  • Click "Create Account" to add the new user.

Editing a User

  • Click the Edit button next to the user (visible to IT Specialists only).
  • Update name, role, or department.
  • Click "Save User" to apply changes.

Deleting a User

  • Click the Delete button next to the user (visible to IT Specialists only).
  • Confirm the deletion.
  • Important: The developer account cannot be deleted.
Note

The User Management page is only visible to users with appropriate permissions (IT Specialist, System Admin, Supervisor).

11. Activity Logs

The Activity Logs page shows a complete audit trail of all user actions in the system.

What is Logged?

  • Login — When users log in to the system
  • Employee Actions — Adding, editing, or deleting employees
  • Attendance Changes — Any changes made to attendance records
  • Report Generation — When reports are downloaded
  • User Management — Creating, editing, or deleting user accounts
  • Profile Updates — When users update their profiles

Filtering Logs

  • Action Filter — Filter by specific action type (e.g., LOGIN, EMPLOYEE_ADD)
  • User Filter — Filter by specific username
  • Date Range — Filter by date range
Note

IT Specialists can see all user activities. Other users can only see their own activities. This is for privacy and security reasons.

Clearing Logs

Users with IT Specialist permissions can clear logs. This is typically done when logs are very old and need to be archived. Be careful with this feature as it removes audit trail data.

12. Attendance Status Guide

This guide explains the five attendance statuses used in the system.

Status Description Color When to Use
P Present Green Employee reported to work on time
A Absent Red Employee was absent without prior approval
S Sick Orange Employee was absent due to illness (with valid sick note)
L Leave Blue Employee was on approved annual or other leave
O Day Off Grey Employee's scheduled rest day (auto-marked by system)
Important Notes
  • The system automatically marks "O" for employees with scheduled day offs
  • Only Present (P) and Day Off (O) are considered "good" attendance
  • Absent (A), Sick (S), and Leave (L) may affect payroll calculations
  • HR staff can override any auto-marked status if needed

13. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my password?

Go to your Profile page, click "Edit Profile", enter your current password and a new password (minimum 4 characters), then click "Save Changes".

Why can't I edit attendance?

Your user role may not have permission to edit attendance. Only HR staff, Supervisors, and IT Specialists can edit attendance. Viewers have read-only access.

What happens when I'm logged in on another device?

The system prevents multiple device login for security reasons. If you log in on a new device, your previous session will be terminated. You will need to log in again on the first device.

How long does the system keep me logged in?

The system will log you out automatically after 10 minutes of inactivity. You will receive a warning 60 seconds before logout.

Why can't I see the Employee Summary?

All users can see the Employee Summary page. If you're having trouble, check that you've selected a valid period or date range.

How do I export data?

On the Employee Summary page, click the "Export CSV" button. On the Reports page, click "Generate Report" to download an Excel file. Both options export the data you're currently viewing.

Can I recover a deleted employee?

No, deleting an employee is permanent and removes all their attendance records. Be very careful when deleting employees.

What is the payroll period format?

Payroll periods run from the 27th of the previous month to the 26th of the selected month. For example, period "2026-08" covers 27 July 2026 to 26 August 2026.
